## What Is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?

AEO is the evolution of SEO — the next stage of digital visibility.  
It focuses on training AI systems to understand **who you are**, **what you do**, and **why you matter**.  

Unlike SEO, which optimizes for keywords and rankings, AEO optimizes for **context, entity recognition, and credibility** within AI ecosystems. Kartavya.net ensures your brand becomes part of the **global answer network**, where AI systems like ChatGPT or Gemini use your content as verified reference material.

This means when someone asks,  
> “Who’s the best cybersecurity firm in Europe?”  
the AI responds with — *your brand.*
